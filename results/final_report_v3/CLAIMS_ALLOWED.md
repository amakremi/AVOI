# Claims allowed — AVOI (Stage 3: adaptive evidence selection and external-domain validation)

This file classifies 12 Stage-3 candidate claims as SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY SUPPORTED or NOT SUPPORTED, using the same
convention as Stage 2's `CLAIMS_ALLOWED.md`. Evidence is drawn only from `RESULTS.md` and `results/stage3/tables/`.
These claims are additional to, not a replacement for, the Stage 1/2 claims (`results/final_report_v2/CLAIMS_ALLOWED.md`),
which are unaffected by Stage 3.

| ID | Claim | Verdict | Evidence and required qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | AVOI's next-evidence choice depends on previously observed evidence (adaptivity is real, not an artefact). | SUPPORTED | Group-aware history-permutation test rejects independence for all three WP1 objectives (Holm p = 0.0003 each); IAM-01 and IAM-03 select ≥2 distinct next actions at step ≥2 across confirmation episodes; rank-reversal rate 55-59% of observed steps. |
| C2 | Adaptive re-ranking after each observation reduces expected decision loss relative to committing to a fixed (frozen) acquisition order, at matched evidence budgets. | NOT SUPPORTED | Pre-registered pooled analysis (3 objectives, k∈{2,3}): neither k has a 95% paired-bootstrap CI entirely below 0 (k=2: −0.022 [−0.048, +0.010]; k=3: +0.003 [−0.038, +0.048]); neither reaches Holm significance. |
| C3 | Adaptive re-ranking helps on at least one objective. | SUPPORTED | IAM-02-TERM-DISABLED: adaptive loss lower than frozen by 0.086 (k=2) and 0.113 (k=3), Holm p = 0.0006 both. Qualification: this is a per-objective, descriptive finding, not the pre-registered primary test (C2). |
| C4 | Adaptive re-ranking is never worse than a frozen order. | NOT SUPPORTED | IAM-03-PRIV-AUTHZ: frozen order is numerically better at both k=2 and k=3, reaching a marginal effect at k=3 (pre-Holm p=0.025, Holm p=0.098). This is the one WP1 objective whose evidence model is adequacy-RESTRICTED; adaptive re-ranking against a miscalibrated value signal can cost loss. |
| C5 | At least one external (non-IAM) evidence model passes the unchanged Stage-2 adequacy policy on independent validation data. | SUPPORTED | CERT-INSIDER: VALIDATED (`ALL_PRIMARY_ADEQUACY_CONDITIONS_MET`), AUROC 0.895 [0.671, 0.953], BSS 0.744 [0.554, 0.918], ECE 0.017 [0.005, 0.064], n=394, 21 violated, 187 independent groups. This is E1, decomposed as E1-CERT supported, E1-AIT not supported. |
| C6 | At least one AIT-LDS v2.0 claim passes the unchanged adequacy policy. | NOT SUPPORTED | AIT-EXFIL and AIT-INTRUSION are both RESTRICTED (`INSUFFICIENT_ADEQUACY_SAMPLE_SUPPORT`) on the target testbed (shaw). | C10 | Target-partition calibration meaningfully improves an external evidence model beyond its zero-shot (development-only-calibrated) form. | PARTIALLY SUPPORTED | CERT-INSIDER: BSS improves 0.488 → 0.744 and ECE improves 0.113 → 0.017 with target calibration, discrimination unchanged (AUROC identical by construction under monotone recalibration). ## Statements that must not appear

- "AVOI adapts and this always improves outcomes" (unqualified) — C2/C4 contradict this; adaptivity can help (IAM-02),
  do nothing (IAM-01) or hurt (IAM-03), depending on the evidence model's adequacy.
- "AVOI now works on AIT-LDS v2.0" or "AVOI generalizes to CERT" — only one CERT claim, on a reduced release pool,
  passed; both AIT claims remain RESTRICTED.
- "The Stage-2 AIT-EXFIL failure proves AVOI cannot transfer to external domains" — it was a fixable calibrator defect,
  not a structural limitation of AVOI or the underlying evidence models.
- "CERT-INSIDER is a reliable insider-threat detector" — it has zero sensitivity to the violated class at its tuned
  threshold; VALIDATED is an adequacy-policy pass, not an operational recommendation.
- "AVOI is more efficient than all baselines on CERT-INSIDER" — the efficiency claim is gated out against All Evidence
  and Information Gain by the pre-registered no-trivial-abstention rule.
- "Stage 3 was part of the original Stage-1/2 pre-registration" — it was designed and frozen afresh in `config/stage3.yaml`
  after Stage 2 concluded, using Stage-1/2 data only as Stage-3 development data.
