# Failure analysis — AVOI, Stage 1 and Stage 2

This file keeps difficult cases in. Supporting material:
- Stage-2 trace excerpts: `results/stage2/failure_cases.md`, generated by `experiments/stage2_failure_cases.py`.
- Adequacy and escalation tables: `results/stage2/tables/`.
- Stage-1 trace-level analysis: `results/stage1_ungated/manuscript/final_report/FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md`.

## 1. Stage-1 AIT failure (historical)

On AIT-LDS v2.0, every method had UAR ≈ 0.31 in Stage 1:
- AVOI: 0.305 [0.273, 0.336].
- All Evidence: 0.314.
- Information Gain: 0.305.

The evidence model was fitted on one testbed and evaluated on the other. Its full-evidence ECE was ≈ 0.26 and its
calibration slope ≈ 0.19, and false assurances were issued at terminal belief ≥ 0.95. No acquisition order could fix
this, which motivated the Stage-2 adequacy condition. Stage-1 numbers are unchanged.

## 2. Poor cross-testbed and cross-release calibration (Stage 2)

- **AIT-EXFIL**, validated on the unused testbed *fox*:
  - AUROC 0.024 [0.013, 0.038], i.e. the ranking is inverted; BSS −0.451; ECE 0.587; slope −6.55.
  - The development-selected sigmoid calibrator (chosen on leave-one-testbed-out predictions) *worsened* ECE from
    0.203 to 0.587.
  - On confirmation (harrison, wardbeck), the All Evidence policy decided 746 episodes autonomously with an error rate
    of 0.71. Realised loss was 1.42 against a model-predicted loss of 0.46.
- **CERT, r4.2 → r5.2**:
  - The model discriminates moderately (AUROC 0.764) but is over-confident: slope 0.28, ECE 0.183.
  - All Evidence on confirmation: autonomous error rate 0.315, realised-minus-predicted loss +2.28.
  - Raw AVOI with partial pre-revealed evidence (missingness runs): UAR 0.29–0.36.
  - Release differences required four label-blind harmonisation rules (`docs/STAGE2_DATA_INDEPENDENCE.md`); these may
    contribute to the shift.
- **IAM-06**: the development-selected *isotonic* calibrator produces posteriors of exactly 0 or 1 for many evidence
  states. AVOI's model-predicted loss for its autonomous decisions was 0.000, while the realised loss was 0.110. The
  model passed the policy (ECE 0.049), but this piecewise-constant calibration is a residual over-confidence risk.

## 3. Small violated-case support

- **AIT-INTRUSION** has 9 violated windows in validation (3 groups with a violation) and 15 in confirmation. It is
  RESTRICTED for sample support; it also fails discrimination (AUROC CI lower bound 0.388) and calibration.
- On confirmation, raw AVOI assured 9 of 15 violations: UAR 0.60 [0.17, 1.00]. Every false assurance had terminal
  belief 0.988. A typical trace is `ait|harrison|1644303600|AIT-INTRUSION`: one acquisition,
  `ait_query_web_error → no_data`, belief 0.982 → 0.988, Assured.
- Calibration slopes of near-separated IAM models have unbounded bootstrap CIs, e.g. IAM-02 [1.02, 129], because
  16 validation families are few.

## 4. Information-gain wins

On VALIDATED IAM confirmation episodes (768), Information Gain versus AVOI:
- **UAR:** identical (0.003).
- **Expected loss:** lower, 0.423 vs 0.440; difference +0.017 [−0.001, 0.035] for AVOI.
- **Coverage:** higher, 0.738 vs 0.677.
- **Per episode:** AVOI's loss is higher in 30 episodes and lower in 17. All 30 are episodes where AVOI acquired
  less evidence.
- **Per objective:** IAM-02 loss 0.167 vs AVOI 0.188; IAM-06 0.531 vs 0.578.
- **Risk-coverage AURC:** 0.0024 vs 0.0023, a tie.
- **Budgeted runs:** Information Gain continues to improve up to k = 6 (0.423), while AVOI stays at 0.440.

Stage-1 H2 ("AVOI has lower UAR than information gain") remains not supported.

## 5. All-evidence wins

- **Stage 1:** All Evidence had lower IAM expected loss (0.316 vs 0.381).
- **Stage 2, VALIDATED IAM:**
  - Expected loss 0.398 vs 0.440 (+0.042 [0.005, 0.082] for AVOI).
  - UAR 0.000 vs 0.003.
  - Balanced accuracy 0.849 vs 0.828.
  - All Evidence has the lower loss on every validated objective: IAM-02 0.167 vs 0.188, IAM-03 0.698 vs 0.745,
    IAM-04 0.203 vs 0.250, IAM-06 0.526 vs 0.578.
- **Price of exhaustive evidence:** 6.00 vs 1.45 acquisitions, and cost 1.546 vs 0.962.
- **Per episode:** AVOI's loss is higher in 70 episodes and lower in 48. All 70 are episodes where AVOI acquired less
  evidence than All Evidence.

## 6. Cases where Random or Cheapest First obtain lower UAR or loss

On VALIDATED IAM models:
- **IAM-06-ACCESS-REVIEW.** Random (seed 0) has UAR 0.000 vs AVOI 0.0096 (one unsafe assurance among 104 violations),
  and lower loss (0.500 vs 0.578).
- **IAM-04-DORMANT.** Cheapest First has lower loss (0.193 vs 0.250).
- **Pooled.** Random (20 seeds) has UAR 0.004 and loss 0.414, versus AVOI's 0.003 and 0.440. The expected-loss
  difference is not significant after Holm correction (p = 0.35).
- **Per episode vs Random seed 0.** AVOI's loss is higher in 51 episodes and lower in 28.

In all these cases the baseline needed more evidence: Random 4.29 and Cheapest First 5.26 acquisitions, versus 1.45 for
AVOI.

## 7. False assurances under conflicting evidence

**IAM (raw AVOI, all six models): 6 false assurances.**
- All six had terminal belief ≥ 0.95, and all six carry the label-free conflicting-evidence flag.
- Five of them come from RESTRICTED models: IAM-05-BREAKGLASS (4) and IAM-01-MFA-PRIV (1). The gate removes these.
- Repeated pattern on IAM-05 (`s2scn-0201`, `-0213`, `-0262`): `recon_register_vs_kc_attribute → consistent` (belief
  0.73), then `kc_breakglass_login_events → no_bg_logins` (0.92), then `host_local_emergency_account → locked` (0.956),
  then Assured.
  - AVOI stopped because the Bayes risk (0.439) fell below the tuned threshold τ = 0.8. Every acquired source supported
    satisfaction, so the conflicting-evidence flag means at least one *unacquired* source showed a violation.
- IAM-01, `s2scn-0207`: one acquisition, `kc_priv_user_credentials → all_priv_have_otp`, belief 0.998, Assured.
  - Here too the conflict flag shows that unacquired sources contained violation-supporting evidence.
- Only one false assurance belongs to a VALIDATED model: IAM-06, 1 of 104 violations.

**AIT-INTRUSION:** 9 false assurances, all at belief 0.988 after an uninformative `no_data` query.

**CERT:** none, because raw AVOI escalated every episode.

## 8. Escalation failures

- **VALIDATED IAM models.** Evidence-uncertainty escalation (248 escalations across all IAM models; 90 with Y=0)
  prevented false non-assurances only: 33 on IAM-02, 70 on IAM-03, 55 on IAM-06. It prevented **zero** false
  assurances, and it withheld correct autonomous decisions (3, 41 and 46 episodes respectively). This replicates the
  Stage-1 H5 caveat.
- **AIT-EXFIL.** Raw AVOI escalated all 1,440 confirmation episodes with **zero acquisitions**: stop reason
  `no_positive_value` at an initial belief of 0.284. The inadequate model made no acquisition look worthwhile.
- **CERT.** All 769 episodes were escalated after a mean of 3.04 acquisitions. Of these, 157 prevented false assurances
  and 106 prevented false non-assurances, but 506 withheld correct decisions. Several escalations occurred after
  evidence had already driven the belief to 0.992 (e.g. `cert|ACM1770|62`, true Y=0). At τ = 0.08 the validation-tuned
  threshold required near-certainty, so a confidently wrong belief was escalated rather than assured. In this case the
  outcome was benign only because the threshold was extreme.

## 9. Zero-acquisition episodes

- **IAM.** P(N_acq = 0) = 0 for AVOI, and every VALIDATED-model episode was acquisition-required.
  - Generic VOI decided 16.7% of episodes and Myopic VOI 33.3% of episodes without any evidence.
  - At the degenerate matched-safety operating points (Section 12), zero-acquisition always-NotAssured policies meet the
    safety and coverage targets.
- **AIT.** Checklist, Cheapest First, Information Gain, Generic VOI, Confidence Threshold and Random all acquire nothing:
  their validation-tuned thresholds terminate at the prior for both micro-claims. Raw AVOI has P(N_acq = 0) = 0.50
  (AIT-EXFIL escalations).
- **CERT.** Every method acquires evidence.

## 10. Harmful or noisy evidence acquisitions

"Harmful" here means an acquisition that moves P(Y=1|E) away from the true label by more than 0.3.
- **IAM (raw AVOI): 91 episodes.** Typical case on IAM-05, true Y = 1 (`s2scn-0139`, `-0217`, `-0284`): after two
  supportive results (belief 0.918), `host_local_emergency_account → unlocked` dropped the belief to 0.264, and the
  decision was NotAssured. This is a false non-assurance on a RESTRICTED model.
- **CERT: 186 episodes.** Typical case, true Y = 1: `cert_device_connects → elevated` moved the belief from 0.662 to
  0.216.
- **AIT: 0 episodes.**
- **Budget non-monotonicity:**
  - AVOI on VALIDATED IAM: 0.432 at k = 2, 0.440 at k ≥ 3.
  - All Evidence: 0.432 at k = 5, 0.447 at k = 6.
  - Generic VOI on CERT: 2.27 at k = 2, 3.08 at k = 3, with UAR 0.447 → 0.638.

## 11. Model inadequacy

Five of nine evidence models are not eligible for autonomous AVOI:

| model | status | failed conditions |
|---|---|---|
| AIT-EXFIL | UNSUPPORTED | discrimination, calibration |
| AIT-INTRUSION | RESTRICTED | support, discrimination, calibration |
| CERT-INSIDER | RESTRICTED | calibration |
| IAM-01-MFA-PRIV | RESTRICTED | calibration slope 1.70 |
| IAM-05-BREAKGLASS | RESTRICTED | calibration slope 4.65 |

AIT and CERT fail under every sensitivity variant of the policy. A public benchmark therefore remains unsupported for
autonomous assurance with the current evidence models.

## 12. The frozen matched-safety definition is still degenerate

- **Design and outcome.** Stage 2 pre-specified a "non-degenerate" matched-safety comparison: minimum cost subject to
  UAR ≤ η AND coverage ≥ γ. It is still degenerate, because coverage counts NotAssured as autonomous.
- **Evidence.** On VALIDATED IAM models, every operating point achieved on validation had zero correct assurances (0
  Assured among Y = 1) for AVOI (48/48), Information Gain (48/48), Checklist (48/48), Cheapest First (48/48),
  Lookahead-2 (48/48) and Random (48/48).
  - The only non-degenerate achieved points belong to All Evidence, Confidence Threshold, Generic VOI and Myopic VOI.
- **Handling.** The definition was not changed after the freeze, and the comparison is reported as uninformative. A
  future protocol must also constrain assurance recall or false non-assurance.
- **Coverage-matched targets.** AVOI met only 3 of 16 targets on validation, and achieved coverage often shifted on
  confirmation.

## 13. Cases where the adequacy gate restricts useful autonomy

| model | raw AVOI UAR | raw AVOI expected loss | coverage | gated expected loss | false assurances prevented | correct decisions withheld |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IAM-01-MFA-PRIV | 0.017 | 0.292 | 1.00 | 1.000 | 1 | 168 |
| IAM-05-BREAKGLASS | 0.038 | 0.312 | 1.00 | 1.000 | 4 | 178 |
| AIT-INTRUSION | 0.600 (9/15) | 0.080 | 0.98 | 1.000 | 9 | 1,406 |

- **IAM-01 and IAM-05.** Both models discriminate almost perfectly, and their realised loss was close to the model's
  own prediction: gaps of +0.05 and +0.01 under All Evidence. The gate reduced unsafe assurance there only by removing
  all autonomy.
- **Sensitivity.** Under a slope interval of [0.25, 1.75], IAM-01 would have been VALIDATED.
- **Handling.** The primary policy stands, and these restrictions are reported as costs of a conservative rule.

## 14. Cases where AVOI spends less evidence but accepts higher decision loss

On VALIDATED IAM models, AVOI's per-episode loss was higher while it acquired less evidence in:
- 70 episodes versus All Evidence;
- 30 versus Information Gain;
- 30 versus Cheapest First;
- 49 versus Random (seed 0).

Pooled, AVOI saves 4.55 acquisitions per episode relative to All Evidence at +0.042 expected loss, and 1.08 relative to
Information Gain at +0.017. Whether this trade-off is acceptable depends on the cost weight λ; it is not a free
improvement.

## 15. Other limitations observed

- **Non-adaptive acquisition order.** Although each IAM objective has 5–7 admissible actions, AVOI selected the same
  first action in every confirmation episode of every IAM objective, then a fixed sequence. IAM-01, IAM-02 and IAM-04
  never went beyond one fixed action. Within an objective, episode-level adaptivity is limited to the stopping point,
  and the heatmap cannot be read as adaptive evidence selection.
- **AIT data.** AIT confirmation used verified subsets of the official archives, not full archives.
- **Stopping and missingness.** With partial pre-revealed evidence, AVOI's IAM UAR rose to 0.031 (MCAR 80%), versus
  0.013 at cold start, while coverage stayed stable. The value-based stopping rule stopped too early on some
  pre-revealed states.
- **Privacy weighting.** Privacy exposure fell only for μ ≥ 0.5, and then at a large loss and coverage cost.
- **LLM planner.** gemma4:e4b reached similar loss to AVOI (0.357 vs 0.372) but with 3× the acquisitions; the
  experiment is restricted to one local model on a subset.
